Students, postdocs,

MOSAIC Lab opens at Aarhus University in December 2026. We welcome enquiries from people interested in structural variation, population genetics, recombination, and human or primate genome evolution.

Future PhD and postdoctoral opportunities will be announced here as they become available. If you are interested in the group’s research, you are welcome to contact Clio with a brief introduction and an explanation of the questions you would like to work on.

Our research questions ↗

Research interests

  • Structural mutation and genome architecture
  • Long-read sequencing and genome assembly
  • Recombination and interlocus gene conversion
  • Population genetics and evolutionary modelling
  • Comparative genomics and archaic introgression

A willingness to learn across these areas is central to the group’s approach.
