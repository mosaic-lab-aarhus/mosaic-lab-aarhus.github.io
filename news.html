November 6, 2026 · Announcement

MOSAIC Lab is coming to Aarhus.

Charikleia (Clio) Karageorgiou will join BiRC, Department of Molecular Biology and Genetics, Aarhus University, as a tenure-track Assistant Professor on December 1, 2026.

The group will investigate how structural mutations arise, why some genomic regions rearrange repeatedly, and how these processes shape human and primate genome evolution.

Our work will combine long-read sequencing, comparative genomics, and population genetics to connect the mechanisms of structural variation with its evolutionary consequences.

Science & community

Sharing the questions.

Public engagement and opportunities for early-career researchers are an important part of Clio’s work. At Buffalo, she established Talk Nerdy to Me, a public science event organized by the UB postdoctoral community. She also co-organized Breaking Bread, a bilingual public outreach event at Barcelona’s Festa Major de Gràcia as part of ESEB 2025.

Her personal website brings together her outreach activities, invited talks, conference contributions, editorial service, and teaching.
