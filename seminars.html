seminars

Conversations on genome structure, population genetics and evolution.

Clio has been organizing this seminar series at the University at Buffalo. The series will now continue jointly between MOSAIC Lab at Aarhus University and the Gokcumen Lab at the University at Buffalo, bringing together researchers in evolutionary genomics, structural variation and population genetics.
